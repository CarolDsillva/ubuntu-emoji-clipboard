Clipboard Picker · browser preview of the Ubuntu app

Pretend this is any app on the laptop

Click somewhere in the text, then press Ctrl+. (this stands in for Super+., which Windows keeps for itself). Copy text here with Ctrl+C to add it to History. You can also paste in something copied from another app: browsers can't watch the clipboard in the background, so pasting is how the preview sees it.
